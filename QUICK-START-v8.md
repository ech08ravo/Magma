# Curriculum Dashboard v8 lite — quick start

No Python or scripts are needed. Works on Mac and Windows.

1. **Unzip** the dashboard ZIP to a folder on your computer. Keep the `lib` folder beside `curriculum-dashboard-v8.html`.
2. **Download from CALM** the reports you have access to into the `CALM-downloads` folder, keeping CALM's file names:
   - `Student Pathways by Units.xlsx` (required)
   - `Units by Assessment Tasks.xlsx` (required)
   - `Course Data.xlsx` (optional; adds course names)
3. **Open** `curriculum-dashboard-v8.html` in Chrome or Edge.
4. Click **⬆ Upload CALM files** and select the files in `CALM-downloads` together. Leave them as `.xlsx`; other CALM files are skipped.
5. Optional: put unit-guide PDFs named with unit codes (for example `SRT141.pdf`) in the `Unit-Guides` folder, check **Also scan a local Unit-Guides folder**, and choose that folder.
6. Check that each file has a green tick, then click **Apply to Model**.
7. Click a unit to see its details. Click **Quick tour** (bottom left) for a short introduction.
8. Optional, for a curriculum map in Excel: in the course's **Assessment timeline**, click **Export Excel**. Open `Curriculum-Map-Builder/Curriculum Map Builder (blank).xlsm`, click **Enable Macros**, click **Import export…** and choose that file. Save the result under your course's name, and keep the blank copy for next time.

Your files are read in the browser and are not uploaded anywhere. After closing or reloading the page, select them again. See `HOW-TO-USE-THE-DASHBOARD-v8.md` for the full guide.
